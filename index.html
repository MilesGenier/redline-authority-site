How Remote Tuning Works

  1. Complete the Vehicle Information Form. On the Remote Tuning page, enter your vehicle, engine setup, modifications, and tuning hardware.
  2. Select your tuning service and submit payment. Choose the service that matches your setup.
  3. Send your current tune file. Email your current HP Tuners or Holley tune file and vehicle details. If you don't have a tune file, contact me before purchasing.
  4. Receive your initial tune. I'll review your setup and calibration, then send a tune file and logging instructions.
  5. Send logs for revisions. Record data logs following the instructions and send them back for review and adjustments.
